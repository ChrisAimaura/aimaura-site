The product

A new way to play.

For years, players have been forced to choose: the precision and rapid aiming of a mouse, or the comfort and familiarity of a controller. Dual Strike ends that compromise. Two ergonomically shaped shells, one for each hand, combine high-resolution mouse tracking with analogue thumbstick navigation, programmable buttons, analogue triggers and a familiar ABXY layout.

  • High-resolution optical mouse sensor on the right shell
  • Analogue joystick & D-pad on the left shell
  • Programmable side buttons, bumpers and triggers on both
  • Wireless RF / BLE with USB-C charging on each unit

What's inside

Every input, exactly where your fingers expect it.

Each shell is built around a one-handed grip. Every button, stick and trigger sits within natural reach, so the device disappears into the way you already play.

Analogue Joystick

Smooth, multi-directional movement under your left thumb, driving on-screen movement and look with the precision you'd expect from a flagship controller.

Directional Pad

A familiar four-way D-pad on the left shell handles menus, weapon swaps and quick-fire navigation, right where your thumb already lives.

ABXY Cluster

The right shell carries a familiar ABXY button layout, ready for jumps, reloads and abilities under your right thumb without taking your hand off the device.

Mouse Tracking Sensor

A high-resolution optical sensor sits beneath the right shell, tracking movement across your desk for the kind of fine aiming a thumbstick simply can't deliver.

Programmable Side Buttons

Three programmable buttons on the inside of each shell. Bind them to anything from melee to push-to-talk for a layout that's truly yours.

Bumper & Trigger

A bumper and an analogue trigger sit along the upper edge of each shell, giving variable input under your index or middle finger for nuanced shooting and acceleration.

USB-C Charging

USB-C on each shell powers the internal battery and supports wired play: wireless when you want it, plugged in when you need it.

Wireless RF / BLE

Each shell transmits independently, with a paired receiver combining both signals into one input device. No proprietary drivers, no perceptible latency.

One-Handed Ergonomics

Every input sits within reach of a single, natural grip. No awkward stretches, no compromised reach. Pick up a shell and start playing.

Industrial design

Sculpted for both hands.

A weighted base, a sculpted thumb crown, a textured grip and a recessed trigger well. Each shell stands ready on the desk and tracks the moment you pick it up.

System architecture

Two shells, one device.

Each shell carries its own microcontroller, battery and wireless transmitter. Their signals are combined by a paired receiver and presented to the host PC or console as a single input device. It behaves as one device to your operating system, while feeling like two natural extensions of your hands.

  • Independent microcontrollers per shell with on-board profile storage
  • Dual RF / BLE receiver with built-in aggregation
  • Standard USB HID output: no drivers, no custom software
